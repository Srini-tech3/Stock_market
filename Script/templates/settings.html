Existing filters

OTM short strike, configured delta band, minimum OI/volume, acceptable bid/ask spread; then minimum POP, credit, RoR and maximum loss per trade.

Spread allowance retains the existing either/or rule: percentage ≤ MaxBidAskPct OR price points ≤ MaxSpreadPoints. Hedge quotes must be valid; hedge liquidity thresholds are not newly imposed.

Important interpretation

Trend, CPRWidth and VIX are context values—not newly added strategy gates. AvailableCapital is not actual broker margin. MinimumCredit/MinimumROR default to zero when omitted, but credit must still be positive and below spread width.

CSV mode uses recorded spot intrinsic values rather than the workbook's SpotPrice. Shared IV is supplied by your CSV; Greeks are imported, not recalculated.
